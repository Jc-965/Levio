# Demonstration video sources

Every clip in this directory is cut from "Exercise, A Video from the
National Institute on Aging" — a production of the National Institute on
Aging, National Institutes of Health. As a work of the United States
federal government it is in the public domain (17 U.S.C. § 105). The NIA
requests credit, which the app shows wherever these clips play:
"Source: National Institute on Aging, NIH."

Clips were transcoded to 480p/30fps H.264 with audio removed. Segment
timestamps, hashes, and retrieval provenance are recorded in the engine
repository under `data/external/public_domain_video/`.

Exercises without a matching public-domain demonstration
(seated march, seated side lean) use the animated guide instead.
